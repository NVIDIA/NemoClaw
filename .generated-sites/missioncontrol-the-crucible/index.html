📱 Essential Safety Tips

Protect yourself and your family from common social media threats.

🔒 Privacy First

Never share your full name, birthdate, or location publicly. Review your privacy settings regularly.

💰 Money Matters

Verify requests for money. Always call or video chat before sending funds to anyone claiming emergencies.

🔐 Strong Passwords

Use a password manager. Enable 2FA everywhere. Never reuse passwords across sites.

👥 Online Connections

Verify identities through multiple channels before trusting online friendships or romantic interests.

⚠️ Urgent Requests

Scammers create urgency. If asked to act immediately, pause and verify through another method.

📱 App Permissions

Review and limit what apps can access your camera, contacts, and other data.
